ABOUT KERALA

Kerala is widely known for its breathtaking natural beauty, often called “God’s Own Country” because of its calm landscapes and rich greenery. One of its most iconic attractions is the backwaters, where peaceful canals, lakes, and rivers flow through villages lined with coconut trees. Houseboats floating on these waters give a unique and serene travel experience. In the hill stations like Munnar, rolling tea plantations stretch across mist-covered mountains, creating a cool and refreshing atmosphere. The greenery and fog together make it look almost like a painting. Kerala’s beaches, such as Kovalam and Varkala, are famous for golden sands, palm trees, and stunning sunsets over the Arabian Sea. These coastal views are both relaxing and visually striking. Beyond nature, Kerala’s beauty also lies in its clean environment, traditional villages, vibrant culture, and peaceful lifestyle. Overall, Kerala is a place where nature, culture, and calmness come together to create unforgettable scenery.
