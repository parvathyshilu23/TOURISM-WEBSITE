Thiruvananthapuram

The capital district, known for temples, beaches like Kovalam, and government institutions.Thiruvananthapuram, commonly known by its anglicised name Trivandrum, is the capital and largest city of Kerala, India. Located on the southwest coast of India, it was famously referred to by Mahatma Gandhi as the "Evergreen City of India" due to its lush, undulating terrain of low coastal hills. It serves as a major political, educational, and technological powerhouse while retaining a relaxed, heritage-steeped atmosphere.
