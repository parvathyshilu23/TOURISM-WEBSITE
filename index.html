KERALA - God's Own Country

INTRODUCTION

Kerala, often referred to as "God's Own Country," is a beautiful state located in the southwestern region of India. Known for its lush greenery, serene backwaters, palm-lined beaches, and rich cultural heritage, Kerala is one of the most popular tourist destinations in the world. Kerala is home to a unique blend of traditions and modernity. The state is famous for its classical art forms like Kathakali and Mohiniyattam, as well as vibrant festivals such as Onam and Vishu. The backwaters of Alleppey and Kumarakom, the hill stations of Munnar, and the wildlife sanctuaries like Periyar make Kerala a paradise for nature lovers. Kerala also stands out for its high literacy rate, strong healthcare system, and progressive social indicators, making it one of the most developed states in India.
